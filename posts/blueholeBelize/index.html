The Great Blue Hole: Belize's Natural Wonder

Kevin Castro May 18 2026 8 min read
The Great Blue Hole, Belize

The Great Blue Hole during sunset

Located approximately 70 kilometers off the coast of Belize, the Great Blue Hole is one of the country's most recognizable natural landmarks. This massive marine sinkhole measures over 300 meters in diameter and plunges more than 120 meters into the depths of the Caribbean Sea. It attracts divers, researchers, and travelers from around the world who come to witness its unique geological formations and breathtaking beauty.

The Great Blue Hole is part of the Belize Barrier Reef Reserve System, a UNESCO World Heritage Site renowned for its rich marine biodiversity. From above, the deep blue circular formation contrasts dramatically against the surrounding turquoise waters, creating one of the most photographed landscapes in Belize.

A perfect circle of deep blue surrounded by crystal-clear Caribbean waters, the Great Blue Hole is one of nature's most spectacular creations.

While many visitors experience the Blue Hole from scenic flights, certified divers can explore its underwater caverns, limestone walls, and ancient stalactites. These formations were created thousands of years ago when sea levels were much lower and the cave system existed above ground.

Diving view of the Great Blue Hole and the surrounding reef system.

The waters surrounding the Blue Hole are home to a variety of marine species, including reef sharks, groupers, and colorful tropical fish. Nearby coral reefs provide excellent opportunities for snorkeling and diving, making the area one of Belize's premier marine destinations.

· · ·

For many travelers, visiting the Great Blue Hole represents more than just a sightseeing opportunity. It is a chance to experience one of the natural wonders that has helped place Belize on the world map. Whether viewed from the air or explored beneath the surface, the Blue Hole showcases the incredible beauty and ecological importance of Belize's marine environment.

As one of the country's most iconic attractions, the Great Blue Hole continues to inspire adventure, curiosity, and appreciation for the natural treasures found throughout Belize.
